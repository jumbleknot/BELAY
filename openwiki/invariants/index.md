- [Final validation checklist](feature-validation-checklist.md) - The sequence of checks every feature or change must pass before it is marked complete and its PR opened — the test-task format, the doc-impact close-out, both scripts Nx targets, the Spec Kit gate when its files changed, the same-PR wiki refresh, and rtk gain last.
- [Nx as the universal task runner](nx-task-runner.md) - Why every test, lint, build, e2e and deploy operation goes through an Nx target rather than the underlying tool, the two scripts targets that are CI's whole check surface, and the deliberate exception for operator CLIs whose arguments vary per invocation.

- [RTK (Rust Token Killer) token compression](rtk-token-compression.md) - RTK is a mandatory transparent CLI proxy that compresses command output before it reaches the agent's context; it must be active before any AI-assisted session, is verified with rtk gain, and can rewrite or hide a tool's answer, so a surprising result is re-run without it.
- [Secrets management posture](secrets-management.md) - No clear-text secrets in git, what secrets/ may hold, the three forge credentials and their deliberately separate scopes, why ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is never set where AI coding runs, and the scan, redactor and never-print rules that keep a credential out of output, digests and wiki pages.
- [Testing tiers and what gates a merge](testing-tiers.md) - The test and gate tiers BELAY has today — node:test unit tests and self-testing gates, both run through Nx and required by the guardrails check — the constitution's unit, integration and E2E definitions a new project inherits, why a skip is treated as a false green, and the rule that a failing guard is fixed at its cause, never deleted.
- [Rules stated but not mechanically enforced — where enforcement is soft](unenforced-rules.md) - The register of BELAY rules that a document states but no gate checks — spec-before-code, RED-before-GREEN, summary accuracy, semantic doc drift, structural decisions, redaction beyond the scanners — with where each is stated, what backs it instead, and the backlog item that would close it; re-read at every quarterly drift audit.
