- [Image CVE scanning — the builder gate and the scheduled sweep](image-scanning.md) - How BELAY scans container images with a pinned, sha256-verified Trivy — the dev-container build scans before push and publishes nothing on a fixable Critical, a Tuesday and Friday sweep covers every third-party image the tree names, the verdict line always counts the images scanned, the sweep/verdict status is the only readable record of a green run, and allowlist entries must be version- or digest-keyed and expiring so a re-pin discharges them.
- [The dependency bot (Renovate) — what it proposes, when, and how it fails loudly](renovate.md) - BELAY's self-hosted Renovate on the belay runner, as the restricted belay-renovate account — it proposes and never merges; a security channel four times a day and a Wednesday window in UTC; every pin managed or excluded, held to rules C1–C11 by check-renovate-config.mjs; version-and-sha256 pairs moved together and verified on every PR; the dashboard kept out of backlog ready; and a run judged from its own log, never its exit code.
